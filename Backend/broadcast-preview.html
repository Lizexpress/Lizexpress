New feature

Obasope, you can now advertise on LizExpress

Until now LizExpress did one thing: swapping. Today we are adding a second — paid advertisements for vendors, shops and service providers.

If you sell anything — food, fabric, phone repairs, catering, tailoring, building materials — you can now put it in front of people searching in your own state, city and local government area.

How it works

1
Open your dashboard and choose Advertise. You can do this alongside swapping — you do not have to pick one.
2
Add your business details and the area you serve: state, city and LGA.
3
Upload photos of what you offer. Each photo costs ₦1,000.
4
Pay once and your advert goes live for 30 days. Customers nearby can find you, see your work and call you directly.
Your swapping account is unchanged. Your listings, chats and saved items all stay exactly as they are — advertising is simply a second thing you can switch on.
